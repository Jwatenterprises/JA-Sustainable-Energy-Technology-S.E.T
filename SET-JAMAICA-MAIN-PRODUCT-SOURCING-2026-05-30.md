# S.E.T. Jamaica Main Product Sourcing: Solar Generators + Solar Panels

Updated: 2026-05-30

Main product lanes:
- Solar generators / portable power stations: sample-first or quote-only depending on size.
- Solar panels: RFQ/procurement only, tied to installed systems or wholesale quote.

No main product should be public checkout until Jamaica shipping/freight, customs treatment, warranty, certifications, support path, and sample or installer review are confirmed.

## Solar Generator Strategy

| Tier | Product class | Use case | Commerce model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SG-1 | 150-300Wh mini station | Phone, router, small lights, emergency charging | Sample-first only; possible low-ticket checkout if shipping and warranty clear |
| SG-2 | 500-1000Wh portable station | Renters, home office, fan/router/lights backup | Sample-first, then quote/order request |
| SG-3 | 1000-2200Wh LiFePO4 station | Home fridge/router/lights, small business POS, clinic backup basics | Quote/order request; do not auto-checkout until warranty and freight path are clear |
| SG-4 | 3000Wh+ LiFePO4/UPS station | SME backup, events, churches/schools, medical-light loads | Quote-only with load review |

Required specs:
- LiFePO4 preferred over ordinary lithium-ion for main SKUs.
- Pure sine wave AC output.
- 110/120V 60Hz or 120/240V Jamaica-compatible configuration.
- MPPT solar input.
- CE/FCC/RoHS/PSE where relevant; UN38.3/MSDS for battery shipping.
- Written warranty and replacement process.

## Solar Panel Strategy

Solar panels should not be ordinary checkout products at launch. Use them for:
- 3 kW / 5 kW / 10 kW installed quote packages.
- Wholesale/procurement quotes for churches, schools, SMEs, farms, and communities.
- Replacement/expansion panels only after installer review.

Required specs:
- Mono PERC, N-Type TOPCon, or bifacial dual-glass depending use.
- Prefer Tier 1 brands or verified OEMs: LONGi, JA Solar, Jinko, Trina, Risen, Bluesun/recognized exporters only after document review.
- IEC 61215 / IEC 61730 mandatory.
- CE/TUV/UL where applicable.
- Factory datasheet must match exact model and label.
- Container/pallet packaging specs and breakage policy required.

## Red Flags To Control

- Do not buy Tier 1 panel claims without authorization proof and exact certificate/model match.
- Do not trust CE/UL/TUV claims without test reports matching the exact panel model.
- Avoid high-wattage panel listings where physical size and cell count do not support the wattage claim.
- Portable generators must be tested under real load before customer sale.
- Lithium battery shipments need UN38.3/MSDS and freight/courier acceptance.
- Warranty is only useful if replacement logistics to Jamaica are written down.
