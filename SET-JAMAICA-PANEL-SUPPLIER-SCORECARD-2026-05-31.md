## Minimum Approval Gate

Before any supplier can move from RFQ to candidate:

- Exact model number and datasheet.
- IEC 61215 and IEC 61730 certificates matching the exact model.
- CE/TUV/UL/CSA documents where applicable, with relevance confirmed.
- Product label photo.
- Warranty document: product warranty and linear power warranty.
- CIF Kingston freight estimate.
- Pallet dimensions, pallet weight, panels per pallet, loading quantity.
- Breakage policy and replacement process.
- Lead time.
- Alibaba Trade Assurance terms.
- Written answer on prior Jamaica or Caribbean shipments.
