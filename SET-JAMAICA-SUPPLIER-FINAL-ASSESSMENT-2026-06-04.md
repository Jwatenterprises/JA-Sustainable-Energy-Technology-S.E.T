## Approval Gate Before Any Supplier Is Used

- Exact model number.
- Exact datasheet.
- Product label photo.
- IEC 61215 certificate matching model.
- IEC 61730 certificate matching model.
- CE/TUV/UL/CSA documents where relevant.
- Product warranty and linear power warranty.
- CIF Kingston freight estimate.
- Panels per pallet, pallet size, pallet weight, container loading quantity.
- Breakage policy and replacement process.
- Lead time.
- Alibaba Trade Assurance scope.
- Prior Jamaica or Caribbean shipment history.
