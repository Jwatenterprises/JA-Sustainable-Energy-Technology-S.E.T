# S.E.T. Sustainable Energy Technology in Jamaica WooCommerce Policy Drafts

Status: draft copy for `shop.setjamaica.com`. Review before public launch.

## Store Notice

S.E.T. Sustainable Energy Technology in Jamaica provides renewable-energy product guidance, quote intake, and selected solar/energy-saving products for Jamaican customers. High-ticket solar systems, batteries, inverters, installation packages, and commercial projects are quote-based and subject to site review, equipment availability, supplier terms, shipping/freight, customs/import handling, installer review, and written quotation.

## Quote-Only Solar Packages

Solar package listings are not final offers, fixed-price contracts, or guaranteed installation commitments. They are starting points for review.

Final recommendations and pricing may depend on:

- Customer location and parish.
- Site visit or photo/video review.
- Roof, structure, wiring, and load requirements.
- Equipment availability.
- Freight and delivery cost.
- Customs/import treatment.
- Installer capacity.
- Warranty and service requirements.
- Written partner or supplier terms.

S.E.T. does not guarantee financing approval, government incentives, tax treatment, utility approval, or customs exemptions.

## Shipping & Delivery

At launch, S.E.T. Sustainable Energy Technology in Jamaica serves Jamaica-focused customers.

For quote-based solar packages, delivery timing is confirmed only after product selection, supplier confirmation, freight review, and written quote approval.

For checkout-ready accessories, estimated delivery times will be shown on the product page or order confirmation where available. Delivery timelines may change because of supplier processing, courier delays, customs inspection, weather, or address access.

## Returns & Refunds

Low-ticket accessory returns are handled according to the supplier, warranty, and product condition.

Before any return is accepted, S.E.T. may require:

- Order number.
- Photos or video of the product issue.
- Packaging condition.
- Delivery confirmation.
- Supplier approval where the item is supplier-fulfilled.

Installed solar systems, custom-ordered equipment, batteries, inverters, panels, and special-order products may not be returnable once ordered, shipped, installed, or customized unless the written quote or warranty terms say otherwise.

## Warranty & Installation

Warranty terms vary by product, supplier, installer, and manufacturer. S.E.T. will communicate known warranty terms before final quote acceptance where available.

Installation work must be handled by qualified professionals. S.E.T. does not route a customer to an installer as an approved partner until partner terms, service capacity, licensing/insurance proof, references, or other vetting items are reviewed.

## Supplier Disclosure

Some products or quote requests may involve supplier, referral, installation, or fulfillment partners. S.E.T. may receive referral fees, supplier margins, or service fees where permitted.

Partner involvement does not change the need for customer review, written quote approval, and proper installer/supplier verification.

## Privacy Summary

S.E.T. collects customer information needed to respond to quote requests, process orders, arrange delivery, support customer service, and evaluate product or installation fit.

Information may include:

- Name.
- Email.
- Phone/WhatsApp.
- Parish or address area.
- Product interest.
- Site or power-use details.
- Order and payment details where checkout is active.

S.E.T. uses this information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, process orders, coordinate supplier or installer review, and provide customer support.
