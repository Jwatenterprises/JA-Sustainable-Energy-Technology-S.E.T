For Jamaicans abroad

Power for family back home.

You can't be there when the lights go out. You can make sure your family's fridge, fans and phones keep running. Buy a power station at the US price and send it home with Dennis Shipping: about 3 days from Florida to their door.

How Shop & Ship works Get sizing help

Two things to get right

  • Buy a 50Hz model. Many US units only charge on 60Hz, and JPS runs at 50Hz. See our picks.
  • Use your family member's details. Dennis needs the receiver's government ID, TRN and an itemized packing list with values, and the name must match the ID exactly.

How it works

  1. Tell us who it's for (optional): their parish and what they need running. We'll suggest a size for free.
  2. Buy it from the US store through our link, at the US price, and have it delivered to you (or anyone you trust) in Florida.
  3. Drop it at Dennis Shipping, Lauderhill (3695-99 NW 15th St, (754) 223-2348) in its original box. Dennis has offices in the Bronx and Brooklyn too.
  4. It goes door-to-door: about 3 days from Florida, with the customs check at their door and a Dennis representative present. Duty and GCT may apply.
Warranty note: many US warranties only cover units bought and used in the US (EcoFlow's says so). If a Jamaican warranty matters more than price, buy from an authorised seller in Jamaica instead.
