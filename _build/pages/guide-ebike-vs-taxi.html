Charging an e-bike for the same trips from JPS costs roughly J$350–530 a month, or nothing from a solar panel. How long until it pays for itself?

The Mooncool POP folding e-bike is US$1,199.99 on Mooncool's US store (checked 18 October 2026). With freight and customs, expect roughly US$1,500–1,700 (≈ J$240K–270K) delivered: Jamaica Customs charges 10% duty on electric motorcycles, and GCT is zero-rated. Against one taxi each way, it pays back in about 26–29 months; against two taxis each way, about 15–17 months. Registration, insurance or a helmet would add to the cost.

Mooncool confirmed in writing that its chargers are rated 110–240V, 50/60Hz, so they work on JPS power. See the e-bikes and trikes.

Charge it from the sun

  • A full EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max charges a POP about 2.3 times. A BLUETTI AC180 charges it about 1.3 times.
  • One 200 W solar panel puts back about 750–900 Wh into the power station on a clear day, enough for a 20 km round trip with room for phones and a fan.
  • Best routine: charge the power station from the sun by day, and the e-bike from the power station at night.
  • Cloudy days can cut solar by 30–70%, so keep JPS as a backup.

Check the road rules first

  • Helmets are required by law on any motorcycle or bicycle (Road Traffic Act 2018, section 74).
  • The Road Traffic Act has no separate e-bike category. It defines a motorcycle as a motor vehicle with fewer than four wheels, and Jamaica Customs classes e-bikes as electric motorcycles, so a throttle e-bike may need registration, a licence and insurance.
  • Ask the Island Traffic Authority what applies to your e-bike before you ride on the road.

Questions

How much does it cost to charge an e-bike in Jamaica?

About J$80 to J$120 per 100 km from JPS power, at roughly J$50 per kWh. A typical 20 km daily commute costs about J$350 to J$530 a month, or nothing if you charge from a solar panel.

Is an e-bike cheaper than a route taxi?

Over time, usually. A 10 km commute each way costs about J$9,700 a month by route taxi. A Mooncool POP delivered to Jamaica costs roughly J$240,000 to J$270,000, so it pays for itself in about 15 to 29 months, depending on your route.

Do I need a licence for an e-bike in Jamaica?

Possibly. The Road Traffic Act 2018 has no separate e-bike category and defines a motorcycle as a motor vehicle with fewer than four wheels. Helmets are required by law. Ask the Island Traffic Authority what applies to your e-bike.

Can I charge an e-bike from a solar generator?

Yes. A full EcoFlow DELTA 2 Max charges a Mooncool POP about 2.3 times, and one 200 W panel can top the power station up each sunny day.

Will a US e-bike charger work on JPS power?

Check the label. Mooncool confirmed its chargers are rated 110 to 240V, 50/60Hz, which suits Jamaica's 110V, 50Hz power.
