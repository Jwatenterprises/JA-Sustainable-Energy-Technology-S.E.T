Set it up in 3 steps

  1. Charge it from the wall. Plug the station into a normal outlet until it's full. Then switch the output to 50Hz once in the settings (the AC200L manual covers it under "Frequency Switching"). Jamaica's grid is 110 V but 50Hz.
  2. Unfold the panel in full sun. Face it toward the sun with no shade on it, then plug its cable into the station's solar input. On a sunny day one 200 W panel puts back roughly 750–900 Wh.
  3. Plug in what you need. Fridge, fans, lights, TV, Wi-Fi router and phone chargers go straight into the station's outlets, using their normal plugs or a heavy-duty extension cord.

Once it's set up, use it everywhere

  • Home: run fans, lights and chargers on daytime sun power to save energy.
  • Emergency backup: keep the fridge, Wi-Fi and phones going when JPS goes out.
  • Camping and beach days: it's portable, so take it with the panel.
  • Farm: lights, power tools and an electric fence charger where there's no outlet.

Safety in 4 lines

  • Never back-feed your house through a wall socket. It can kill a JPS line worker. Wiring a station into the house needs a transfer switch fitted by a licensed electrician.
  • Keep the station indoors, dry and ventilated; only the panel goes outside.
  • Bring the panel in before a storm.
  • A US warranty may not cover use in Jamaica. More on warranties.

Questions

Do I need an electrician to set up a portable power station?

No. You charge it, plug a solar panel into it, and plug your appliances into its outlets. You only need a licensed electrician if you want it wired into the house through a transfer switch.

Will a US power station work in Jamaica?

Only if it can run at 50Hz. The BLUETTI AC200L can; set it to 50Hz once in the settings. See which models work.

How long does one solar panel take to recharge it?

One 200 W panel puts back roughly 750 to 900 Wh on a sunny day, about 40% of an AC200L. Three panels can refill it on a sunny day.

Can it run my air conditioner?

Not through the night. For all-night AC or a water pump, you need a much bigger battery bank.
