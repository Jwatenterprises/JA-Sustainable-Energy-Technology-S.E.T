Built for Jamaica's grid

Jamaica is 110V like the US, but 50Hz, not 60Hz.

The plugs and voltage match US gear, so it's easy to assume a US power station will just work. But several popular US models are only rated to charge on 60Hz power. On JPS's 50Hz grid, that can mean overheating, faults and a voided warranty.

We check both the charging input and the AC output of every unit before it goes on our shelf.
