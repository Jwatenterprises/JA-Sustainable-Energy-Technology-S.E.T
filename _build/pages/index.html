Why "50Hz-verified" matters

Same plug, different power.

Jamaica uses US-style plugs at 110V, but the grid runs at 50Hz. Many US-spec power stations output 60Hz and some won't charge properly on JPS. Every station we sell must output 110–120V at 50Hz, charge on 100–240V 50/60Hz, and pass a bench test on JPS power in Kingston.
