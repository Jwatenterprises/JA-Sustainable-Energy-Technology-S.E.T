What we collect

When you message, email, reserve or order, we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, email, delivery address and parish, and order details. Payment is handled by your bank or PayPal; we don't store card numbers.

How we use it

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the delivery partner or payment provider needed to complete your order, or where the law requires it.
