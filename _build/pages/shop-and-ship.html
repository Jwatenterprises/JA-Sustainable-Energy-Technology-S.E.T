What Dennis needs from the receiver

  • Valid government ID. The receiver's name must match it exactly.
  • TRN (Tax Registration Number).
  • Itemized packing list with contents and values.
  • Personal use only. Buying stock to resell needs a business licence.

How it works

Five steps from the US store to your house.

  1. Pick a model that works on JPS power. Only units rated for 110V at 50Hz. Our 50Hz picks are checked against the manufacturers' manuals.
  2. Buy it from the US store through our link. You pay the store directly, at the US price. Have it delivered to a Florida address you trust: family, a friend, or your own. (Ask Dennis whether they can receive it at their Lauderhill office for you.)
  3. Drop it at Dennis Shipping in Lauderhill, in its original retail box. Dennis bills by package size, not weight, so a heavy battery costs no more than a light box the same size.
  4. Door-to-door to Jamaica. Customs is checked at delivery with a Dennis representative present, so the receiver doesn't have to go to the wharf. Duty and GCT may apply; ask Dennis or a customs broker what to expect before you ship.
  5. Set it up for 50Hz and test it. The EcoFlow DELTA 2 and DELTA 2 Max handle 50Hz automatically. On the BLUETTI AC180, hold the AC and DC buttons for 2 seconds, then press AC to switch to 50Hz. Run your fridge on it for an hour before storm season.

Before you buy

The warranty question.

Many US warranties only cover units bought and used in the United States. EcoFlow's US warranty says exactly that. A US unit is usually the lower price; a unit from an authorised seller in Jamaica (for EcoFlow, that's ATL) usually comes with a local warranty. Pick the trade-off that suits you.

Do I pay S.E.T.?

No. You pay the store.

There's no S.E.T. fee. You buy directly from the US store and pay Dennis for shipping. When you use our links, the store pays us a small commission at no extra cost to you. That's how we keep this guide free.

Questions

Will Dennis really ship a lithium power station?

Yes. Dennis Shipping confirmed to us in October 2026 that they accept lithium power stations and batteries to Jamaica for personal use. Keep the unit in its original box, and confirm any packing rules with the office when you drop it off.

Can I ship from New York?

Dennis has offices in the Bronx and Brooklyn. The Thursday sailing and Monday/Tuesday arrival above are for the Florida office, so ask the New York office for its own schedule and rate.

How much does shipping cost?

Dennis prices by box volume, and the rate changes daily. On October 20, 2026, their Florida office quoted about US$80 to Kingston (US$95 to Montego Bay) for a DELTA 2 Max box, and about US$55 (US$65–70) for a BLUETTI AC180 box, and about US$60 to Kingston for a BLUETTI 200W solar panel box. Call with your box size for today's price.

Why not buy any US power station?

Many popular US units only charge on 60Hz power, and JPS runs at 50Hz. Charging outside the rating can damage the unit and void the warranty. See which models work.

I live abroad. Can I send one to family?

Yes, that's what Shop & Ship is built for. Buy in the US, drop it at Dennis, and put your family member's name, ID and TRN on the paperwork. See Send power home.
