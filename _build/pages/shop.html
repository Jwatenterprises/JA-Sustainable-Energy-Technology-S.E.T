E-bikes & trikes

Electric bikes and trikes for Jamaica

Mooncool confirmed in writing (Oct 17, 2026) that the chargers for these models are rated 110–240V~ 50/60Hz, so they charge on JPS power. Charge from the wall or from a solar power station.

Read before you buy: Mooncool's warranty is for the US only and does not cover bikes used in Jamaica. Replacement parts ship to US addresses only, so you would forward them yourself. Prices are in US dollars, and your bank may add currency or foreign-transaction fees. A bike box is large, so ask your shipper for the price by box size first. Check Jamaica's current road rules for e-bikes before riding on public roads.

E-scooters

Electric scooters for Jamaica

A low-cost way to get around on short, paved commutes. Each charger is rated 100–240V, 50–60Hz in the maker's manual, so it charges on JPS power. Every pick is UL 2272 certified, with 10-inch-plus tubeless tyres and suspension for our roads.

Safety first: wear a helmet on every ride. We encourage you to get one with your scooter, like this CPSC-certified helmet with front and rear lights.

Read before you buy: The Island Traffic Authority (ITA) and local authorities decide whether an e-scooter needs registration, insurance or a licence, so check with them before riding on public roads. Dennis Shipping ships lithium-battery goods; you are responsible for declaring the scooter's lithium battery when you ship it. How to declare: ship it in the original box, switched off and charged to about 30% or less, and list the model and battery watt-hours (Wh) on your packing list. Segway's warranty covers North America only, and NIU's US warranty does not cover Jamaica. Prices are in US dollars.
