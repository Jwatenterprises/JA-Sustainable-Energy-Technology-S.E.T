Goods are released once payment has cleared. For layaway, the product stays with us until the final payment.

Delivery times are estimates. We'll keep you updated if anything changes. Please check your order on delivery and tell us about any damage right away.

Warranty terms are shown on each product page and on your invoice. See Warranty & returns.
