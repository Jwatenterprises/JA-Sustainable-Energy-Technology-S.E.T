Warranty & returns

Warranty handled here in Jamaica

If something goes wrong, you deal with us in Jamaica, not a call centre overseas or a battery you have to ship back to the US.

Warranty

  • Every product comes with a written warranty. The length is shown on each product page and on your invoice.
  • Claims are handled locally by S.E.T. Message us on WhatsApp or email with your invoice number, and photos or a short video of the problem.
  • We test, then repair or replace under the product's warranty terms.
  • Warranty doesn't cover damage from misuse, water or flooding, physical damage, or unauthorised repairs or modifications.

Faulty on arrival

Every unit is checked before it leaves us. If a product doesn't work when you first set it up, contact us right away and we'll replace it.

Returns

If you change your mind, contact us before opening or using the product and we'll explain the options. Batteries and power stations that have been used can't be returned for a refund, but they're still covered by the warranty.

Before you call: quick checks

  • Is the unit charged? Try charging from the wall for 30 minutes.
  • For power stations: is the AC output switched on, and set to 50Hz?
  • Is the load too big? Check the watts against the unit's rating (see our sizing guide).
