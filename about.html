About us

Power you control, built for Jamaica.

S.E.T. Sustainable Energy Technology in Jamaica sells solar batteries, power stations and everyday solar gear, stocked in Kingston.

Jamaicans pay some of the highest electricity rates in the region, and Hurricane Melissa showed how quickly the grid can go down for weeks. Backup power shouldn't mean a noisy gas generator, a courier battery that may never arrive, or a US unit that isn't rated for our 50Hz grid.

What we stand for

  • Built for Jamaica's grid. Every AC product is checked for 110V / 50Hz before we stock it.
  • Honest specs. Real capacity and real wattage, not inflated "1000W" labels.
  • Tested before sale. We bench-test samples on JPS power.
  • Local warranty. If something goes wrong, you deal with us here.
  • Clear J$ prices. All-in, with no surprises at the door.
