About us

Power you control, built for Jamaica.

S.E.T. Sustainable Energy Technology in Jamaica helps you buy backup power that works on Jamaica's grid, at US store prices, and get it home door-to-door.

Jamaicans pay some of the highest electricity rates in the region, and Hurricane Melissa showed how quickly the grid can go down for weeks. Backup power shouldn't mean a noisy gas generator or a US unit that isn't rated for our 50Hz grid.

What we stand for

  • Built for Jamaica's grid. We only recommend models rated by their manufacturer for 110V / 50Hz.
  • Honest specs. Real capacity and real wattage, not inflated "1000W" labels.
  • Straight answers on warranty. We tell you when a US warranty won't cover Jamaica.
  • No fee. You buy from the store; we earn a commission from our links.
