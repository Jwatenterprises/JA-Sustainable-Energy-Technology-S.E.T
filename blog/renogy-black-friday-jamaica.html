Questions

Will the Renogy 3500W inverter work on Jamaica's 50Hz?

Yes, once it's set. Renogy's specifications list the input/output frequency as 50/60Hz adjustable and the output voltage as 120V, adjustable from 100V to 120V in 5V steps. Set it to 50Hz and 110V before connecting appliances.

Do Renogy solar panels and batteries work in Jamaica?

Yes. Panels, charge controllers and lithium batteries are DC equipment, so the grid frequency doesn't affect them.

Does Renogy ship to Jamaica?

Renogy's US store sells to US addresses, so most Jamaicans buy to a Florida shipping address and send it home with a shipper like Dennis. See how Shop & Ship works.
