For Jamaicans abroad

Power for family back home.

You can't be there when the lights go out. You can make sure your family's fridge, fans and phones keep running. Pay in US dollars from abroad, and we deliver to their door in Jamaica.

Why not ship it from the US?

  • Lithium batteries are dangerous goods. Many barrel shippers and couriers won't take them.
  • Many US units aren't rated for Jamaica's 50Hz grid. See the list.
  • US warranties often don't cover Jamaica. Ours is handled here.
  • No duty or customs surprises for your family at the door.

How it works

  1. Tell us who it's for: their parish and what they need to keep running (fridge, fans, CPAP, phones).
  2. We recommend the right size and send you a USD total including delivery.
  3. Pay by PayPal in US dollars from abroad.
  4. We deliver and show them how it works, charged and ready, and message you when it's done.
First stock lands in December, in time for Christmas and well ahead of next hurricane season. Reserve now; no payment until it's in Kingston.
