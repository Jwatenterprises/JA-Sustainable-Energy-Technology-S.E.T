Hurricane season guide

Hurricane power checklist for Jamaican homes

By the S.E.T. Jamaica team · Atlantic hurricane season runs 1 June to 30 November

The lesson from Hurricane Melissa: when it struck in October 2025, more than 70% of JPS customers (over 500,000) lost power, and some areas waited weeks for restoration. The homes that coped best had three things ready before the storm: charged batteries, a way to recharge without the grid, and a plan for the fridge and medical devices.

Before the season (May–June)

  • List your must-run devices: fridge, fans, lights, phones, router, CPAP or other medical equipment. Then size your backup.
  • Get backup power in place early. Stock and delivery get tight once a storm is named.
  • Test your power station or battery at home: run your fridge on it for an hour so you know it works.
  • Check it's 50Hz-rated if it plugs into JPS power. Here's why.
  • Buy a solar panel to recharge if outages could last more than a day.
  • Save key numbers (JPS, ODPEM, your doctor, family) on paper as well as your phone.

When a storm is 3–5 days out

  • Charge every power station, power bank and battery to 100%.
  • Charge phones, laptops, rechargeable fans and lamps.
  • Turn the fridge and freezer to their coldest settings and fill empty space with bottles of water. A full, cold freezer holds temperature longer.
  • Download offline maps and important documents to your phone.
  • Bring solar lights, panels and cameras inside or secure them before the wind picks up.

During the outage

  • Unplug sensitive electronics from the wall to protect them from surges when power returns.
  • Run the fridge in cycles (for example, a few hours on, then off) to stretch your battery, and keep the door shut.
  • Prioritise: medical devices first, then the fridge, then fans and lights, then entertainment.
  • Recharge with solar during daylight once the storm has passed and it's safe to set up a panel outside.
  • Gas generators: never indoors, in a garage or near windows. Carbon monoxide kills. A battery power station is safe to use inside.

After the storm

  • Wait a few minutes after JPS power returns before plugging appliances back in.
  • Recharge your backup to 100% right away. Outages often repeat while the grid is being repaired.
  • Check panels and cables for damage before using them.
