Ordering

How to order from S.E.T. Jamaica

No account, no checkout forms. Talk to a real person on WhatsApp, pay the way that suits you, and pick up or get it delivered.

Before stock lands (December): reserve on WhatsApp or by email and we'll message you first with the price. We don't take any payment until your item is in Kingston.

Step by step

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with the product, quantity and your parish. Every product page has a button that fills this in for you.
  2. Get your total. We confirm stock, the J$ price and delivery cost, and send you an invoice.
  3. Pay by bank transfer, PayPal (USD) or at pickup.
  4. Collect or receive your order. We check every unit before it leaves and show you how it works.

Pickup and delivery

  • Kingston pickup: collect in person, see a demo and get set up.
  • Island-wide delivery: priced by parish. Small items go by courier; batteries and power stations go by our delivery partner, since lithium batteries need careful handling.
  • We confirm the delivery cost before you pay. No surprises.

Invoices and GCT

Businesses and installers can request a full invoice for their records. Ask when you order.
