How to order

You buy from the US store. We help you get it right.

S.E.T. Jamaica doesn't sell or hold stock. We tell you which US power stations work on Jamaica's 50Hz grid and how to ship them home. You pay the store and the shipper directly, and there's no S.E.T. fee.

  1. Choose a model from our 50Hz picks, or ask us on WhatsApp for free sizing help.
  2. Buy it through our link from the US store and have it delivered to a Florida address you trust.
  3. Ship it with Dennis Shipping from Lauderhill: door-to-door to Jamaica, billed by box size. Ask them for the current transit time. The receiver needs a government ID, TRN and an itemized packing list.
  4. Set it to 50Hz if your model needs it, and test it at home.
