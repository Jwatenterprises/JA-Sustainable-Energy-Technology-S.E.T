Why "50Hz-verified" matters

Same plug, different power.

Jamaica uses US-style plugs at 110V, but the grid runs at 50Hz. Many US-spec power stations output 60Hz and some won't charge properly on JPS. Every pick we list is rated by its manufacturer to charge on 50Hz and to output 110–120V at 50Hz.
