What we do

S.E.T. Jamaica publishes recommendations and guides for backup power that works on Jamaica's 50Hz grid. We don't sell products or hold stock. When you buy, you buy directly from the store you choose, under that store's terms, and you ship with the shipper you choose, under its terms.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, the store pays us a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. This never changes which products we recommend.

Prices and specs

Prices shown are what the store listed on the date we checked, and they change often. Always confirm the price and specifications on the store's page before you buy. We base our 50Hz picks on manufacturers' published manuals and spec sheets.

Shipping and customs

Shipping is arranged by you with the shipper, such as Dennis Shipping. Their rates, rules, transit times and customs requirements apply. Duty and GCT may apply on arrival in Jamaica. S.E.T. is not affiliated with Dennis Shipping.

Warranty

Warranty is provided by the manufacturer or seller under its own terms. See Warranty.

Safe use

Use products as described in their manuals. Lithium batteries and power stations must be kept dry, ventilated and away from heat sources, and must not be opened or modified.
